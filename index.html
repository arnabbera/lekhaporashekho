Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear your queries regarding our batch timings, course fees, structure, and home tuition support.

What is the student-to-teacher ratio in Arnab Sir's batches?
To guarantee focused attention, our physical batches at Golpark are strictly restricted to 8-10 students. This allows Arnab Sir to personally inspect worksheets, spot individual weaknesses, and clear doubts in real-time.
Does Arnab Sir provide Home Tuition classes?
Yes, premium 1-on-1 home tutoring slots are available for selected regions across South Kolkata (such as Ballygunge, Gariahat, Golpark, Jodhpur Park, and Dhakuria). Because slots are limited due to Sir's schedule, prior booking via our form is advised.
How does the Computer Science department organize practice?
For computer science (Java/BlueJ & Python), students are taught logic building on paper first (dry running code outputs). Subsequently, hands-on coding classes are scheduled where students write programs and debug errors on laptops under direct supervision.
Are there separate batches for different school boards (ICSE vs CBSE vs WBBSE)?
Yes. WBBSE, CBSE, and ICSE follow distinct math and CS syllabi. To ensure targeted academic readiness, our batches are categorized according to boards, aligning with school progress cards and scheduled examinations.
